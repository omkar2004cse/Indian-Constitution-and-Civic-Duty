Right to Equality

Article 14

What does Article 14 of the Indian Constitution guarantee to all citizens?

Click to reveal answer

Answer

Article 14 guarantees equality before law and equal protection of laws to all persons within the territory of India.

Key Point: This means the state cannot deny any person equality before the law or equal protection of laws, ensuring no discrimination based on religion, race, caste, sex, or place of birth.
